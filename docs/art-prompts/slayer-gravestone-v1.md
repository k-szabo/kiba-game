## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production isolated environment prop for a painted Japanese dark-fantasy 2D game, TRUE TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND. The supplied reference is an approved concept sheet; use ONLY the central dark gravestone from the upper-right graveyard scene as the design reference. Draw ONE complete EMPTY upright black basalt gravestone, slightly irregular chipped rectangular headstone with a broad gently uneven FLAT TOP so a small rabbit plush can later sit on it in game; low rubble footing, weathered rope partly around the sides, restrained fine fissures and grey-violet stone edge lighting. NO rabbit, no weapons, no crossbow, no lettering, no inscription, no symbols, no skull or carved faces, no candle, no giant floating magical effect. About 2 times taller than its broad base, solid weighted silhouette, front with a slight three-quarter view matching a side-view game prop. Detailed brush-painted rock texture, dark grey centre but readable grey edges against a dark purple background, a few muted rusty orange reflections on side edges, subtle smoky purple stone colours. Full silhouette and footing inside generous empty transparent margins, no scene behind it, no floor plane, no frame, no cast shadow beyond base. Centered asset on 1024x1024 transparent canvas. It must remain an EMPTY tombstone; inventory rewards will be separately rendered.

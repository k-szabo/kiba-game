# Westheru storm effects

Approved motion: a teal ground-level gust builds, sweeps in one direction and dissipates. Amber markers indicate three staggered impacts; a violet meteor falls diagonally, bursts at its marker and leaves embers and smoke. The scenery stays fixed.

Production prompt: “Production VFX sprite atlas for painterly Japanese fantasy side scrolling game, TRANSPARENT background clean alpha, landscape 3:2 aspect 3 columns by 2 rows regular equally spaced cells, absolutely no text, borders, characters, scenery, labels or terrain, each single isolated centered asset contained inside its own cell with generous padding. Top left: broad shallow horizontal wind gust pointing RIGHT, layered bright turquoise and pearl ribbon streaks, strongly horizontal; top center: same wind gust peak with rolling turquoise curls, glowing white streaks and subtle dust; top right: dissipating wisps with thinner horizontal transparent curling edges. Bottom left: single violet-blue black meteor with orange hot rim, pointed trail extending towards upper left and leading glowing rock pointing down-right; bottom center: upward spreading compact meteor impact with orange hot embers, violet rock shards and dark smoke, base in center; bottom right: fading low dark purple smoke and orange drifting embers from the impact. Silhouettes readable at game scale. Real alpha everywhere outside the isolated effects, NO checkerboard rendered into image. Detailed hand-painted sprite quality.”

The runtime uses the same atlas, crop rectangles and collision geometry in both modes. The fall is decorative; only the marked landing area deals damage.

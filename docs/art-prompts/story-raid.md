# The Second Ziggurat art prompts

- Environments. Paint three wide Japanese fantasy game backgrounds with a continuous walkable foreground. The Third Clan halls use layered stone, wood and cool cave mist. The siege terrace opens onto a stormy ziggurat with warm siege fires. The inner sanctuary uses violet light, monumental masonry and a central seal. Leave the foreground clear for boss silhouettes and warnings.
- Background phases. Keep the original projection, architecture and floor fixed. Paint a second phase with shifted low cave mist, high terrace clouds or violet sanctuary light. Blend only the corresponding region from the second phase.
- Third Clan atlases. Light hood and empty hands for the Mist Sentry. Keep the same small bell on the Signal Keeper. Give the clan leader readable hand seals and a strong stance. Match approved painted proportions across neutral, movement and combat poses.
- Siege and sanctuary atlases. Preserve the shield of the Siege Warden, spell gestures of the Kiba Binder, full spear of the War Marshal, silhouette of the winged Rift Sentinel and one chained censer of the Sanctuary Custodian throughout their poses. Give the transformed Mimic one distorted silhouette and a visible free grip so the game can attach the equipped weapon. The first phase uses Momo's existing equipment and motion.
- Rescue props. On a transparent sheet paint one heavy carved slab and one distinct open gateway lit from Heaven. Paint Reika separately in her established indigo and gold clothing, planting both feet and lifting both palms to hold the slab. Stage Haru's crossing using the existing elder sprite.
- Reward icons. On a transparent sheet paint the three full soul silhouettes with ample margins. Use blue hand-seal wisps for the clan leader, ember-red siege fragments for the War Marshal and violet shifting light for the demon lord.

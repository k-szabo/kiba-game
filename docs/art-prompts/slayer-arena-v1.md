# slayer-arena-v1

Production static art; approved 2026-10-20 from the Démonölő concept, with the separately approved crossbow replacement. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. No source-image postprocessing; generated alpha preserved for props.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production bitmap BACKGROUND for a 2D side-view Japanese dark-fantasy game. Generate a wide 3072 x 1536 landscape painting. First reference is an APPROVED concept sheet: use ONLY its upper-right volcanic Japanese graveyard arena environment as the visual direction. Second reference is the existing Yomi world painting: match its dark basalt detail, aged torii/shrines, muted lavender cavern atmosphere and restrained distant orange lava lighting. This is one large environment background, no concept-sheet layout. We need a broad, quiet, flat arena suitable behind a continuous horizontal gameplay platform: vast ruined Japanese underworld shrine terrace inside a cavern, distant jagged basalt pinnacles and hanging stalactites, weathered torii gates and fragmented shrine roofs at the outer edges and in far distance, shrine ropes in upper margins, purple smoke filling the deep valley, tiny distant lava waterfalls and a dull warm glow well BEHIND the arena. Clear spacious center. The near plane is low-contrast charcoal grey, with a simple horizontal weathered-stone terrace hinted at the very bottom 12 percent; game will render its own traversable ledge, so do not paint obstacle-shaped foreground objects. Camera is fixed frontal side-game background with atmospheric depth, no steep perspective corridor. Most image is background wall/valley and sky-like purple cavern air. No focal grave, no large tombstone, no barrels, no rabbit, no crossbow, no weapons, no characters, no creatures, no loot, no writing or legible symbols, no UI, no vignette black frame. Detailed confident painterly textures consistent with the existing game, broad sombre readable fighting area, no bright foreground lava beneath players, no special attack effects. Do not copy any character or objects from the concept reference. Full bleed opaque image, natural complete scene.

## Registration

Integrator owns runtime metadata, placement, colormaps and browser verification. Background and props are separate; rewards are never baked into the gravestone.

Output: `assets/images/backgrounds/slayer-arena-v1.png`, opaque RGB, 1774 × 887. The tool returned this native size despite the requested 3072 × 1536; no upscaling was applied. Keep small character telegraphs independent of this detailed background.

# Sky Dragon raid

## Adult dragon sprite sheet

The production sheet uses the approved four-panel skyport, pursuit, dragon-back and floating-island concepts and the approved twelve-panel motion storyboard as references. The original alpha is preserved in `assets/images/enemies/sky-raid-dragon-v1.png`.

> Production game sprite atlas of only the adult Sky Dragon from references, landscape, three columns by two rows. Same adult oxidized-turquoise dragon, swept ivory horns, ivory cloud wing feathers with rust highlights, two wings, four legs and one continuous tail in every pose. Always facing right. Full silhouettes, transparent margins, no labels, scenery, characters or effects. Stable torso registration. Upper row: wings high in level flight, downstroke in level flight, banking dive. Lower row: recoiling in flight, grounded injured with wings held over the back and subtle violet corruption on the shoulder, grounded raising its head to cast. No exposed wounds. True alpha backdrop.

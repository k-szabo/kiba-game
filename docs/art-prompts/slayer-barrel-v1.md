# slayer-barrel-v1

Production static art; approved 2026-10-20 from the Démonölő concept, with the separately approved crossbow replacement. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. No source-image postprocessing; generated alpha preserved for props.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production isolated oil-barrel prop for painted Japanese dark-fantasy 2D game. TRUE TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND. The reference approved concept sheet contains wooden oil barrels at the outer sides of the volcanic graveyard arena; match that style. Draw ONE intact upright weathered Japanese wooden stave barrel, broad and squat, slightly bulging centre, old warm dark wood and three thick blackened iron hoops with rivets, closed circular wooden lid visible in slight elevated front-three-quarter perspective, a small wooden bung just above middle. This barrel will later be punctured and ignite in gameplay, but this static source must be INTACT, no hole, no spill, no fire. Fine woodgrain, chipped worn iron, restrained bronze-orange reflections from the right and cool lavender rim on left, high-detail confident hand-painted game texture, easy silhouette recognition at 60px screen height. Whole bottom and full lid visible, complete centered object with generous transparent empty margins, 1024x1024 canvas. No scenery, no ground or cast shadow, no inscriptions, no text, no logo, no character, no skulls, no extraneous weapons, no modern oil drum, no metal drum, no second barrel.

# Ninja Clan dungeon and camp

Reference: the approved six-panel concept. The ninja leader and sentinel use their existing sprite sheets. The leader’s corruption uses the existing void effects and a tint applied to the original sprite.

Background atlas prompt: Create production game background atlas following the first four environment concepts. Exact 2×2 equal quadrant layout with hard straight boundaries at midpoint, no margins or dividers. Each quadrant is a side-scrolling environment with scenic detail in the middle and far distance, no characters, lettering, foreground gameplay platforms or UI. Top left: floating Japanese mountains and stockade in twilight. Top right: ruined ninja stockade with amber lanterns and violet corruption. Bottom left: underground ritual cave with a vertical violet rift and stone shrine. Bottom right: friendly ninja village with a forge, healer tent and tethered balloon. Hand-painted Japanese fantasy matte paintings with distinct warm and cool lighting.

The browser crops the quadrant at render time. The foreground floor and its collision geometry are separate from the painting.

Rift closure: Keep the atlas layout, cave steps, lights, banners and columns. Replace the violet tear with solid stone and leave dim residual reflections. The game blends the closed cave panel in when the ritualist is pulled away.

Ritualist: Transparent six-cell sheet from the approved movement sketch. Idle, forward step, sword raised, slash, ritual casting and stagger. Keep one drawn katana and one scabbard throughout. The healer and smith are static, separately registered figures on their own transparent two-cell atlas.

Inventory: Three separately registered transparent cells based on the approved concept. The leader's soul is a pale blue cat-eared wisp with a violet core. The carved jade seal has a copper loop and violet fractures. The sellable clan spoils are folded indigo fabric tied with straw cord, with copper emblems and coins.
